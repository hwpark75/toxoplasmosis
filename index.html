Why Now

The Inflection Point for
Toxoplasmosis Vaccines & Diagnostics

Five forces converging to create a time-sensitive acquisition opportunity in an entirely unaddressed pathogen.

1
Commercial Demand Proven, Yet Major Vaccine Markets Remain Unaddressed
MSD successfully commercialized Toxovax for sheep, demonstrating real-world market demand. Yet despite 2.5 billion global infections, no approved toxoplasmosis vaccine exists for cats or humans — the largest addressable segments remain entirely unserved.
2
Global Cat Ownership Is Accelerating the Transmission Risk
An estimated 600 million cats globally are the primary transmission vector. Rising pet ownership — particularly in Asia and North America — is structurally expanding the at-risk population. This is a permanent demand driver, not cyclical.
3
Korea and Europe Are Moving Toward Mandatory Screening
The Korean Medical Association proposed mandatory toxoplasmosis screening for pregnant women in 2020. Europe already mandates screening in several countries. Regulatory movement toward mandated diagnostics is the single most important demand catalyst — and it is already underway.
4
Diagnostics Commercialization Pathway is Nearer-Term
ELISA diagnostic kits provide a more immediate regulatory and commercialization pathway, while vaccine programs continue to mature through preclinical and clinical development — creating a staged, de-risked revenue profile.
5
Public Health Adoption Represents Additional Upside Potential
Mandatory feline vaccination programs (e.g., rabies) demonstrate how companion animal vaccines evolve into broader public health tools. Given toxoplasmosis risks to pregnant women and immunocompromised individuals, future public health initiatives could further expand the long-term market opportunity.
